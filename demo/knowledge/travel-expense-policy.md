# Enterprise Travel and Expense Policy

## 1. Scope

This policy applies to every employee, contractor, and approved guest who travels on
company business. It covers flights, rail, accommodation, ground transport, meals, and
expense reporting.

## 2. Booking rules

- Economy class is required for flights shorter than six hours of scheduled flight time.
- Premium economy may be approved for flights between six and ten hours.
- Business class requires written approval from a vice president before booking.
- Rail travel replaces flights when the total rail journey is shorter than five hours.
- All bookings are made through the corporate travel desk unless the desk confirms it
  cannot serve the route within two business days.

## 3. Accommodation

- The standard nightly limit is 220 USD for domestic travel and 320 USD for
  international travel.
- A nightly rate above the limit requires a documented reason and director approval.
- Accommodation is booked for the nights required by the business schedule only.

## 4. Ground transport and meals

- Public transport, ride sharing, and standard rental cars are reimbursable.
- The daily meal allowance is 65 USD domestic and 95 USD international.
- Alcohol is not reimbursable unless it is part of a pre-approved customer event.

## 5. Expense reporting

- Expense reports are submitted within fourteen days of travel completion.
- Receipts are required for every single expense above 25 USD.
- Reports submitted after thirty days require an explanation from the traveler's manager.
- Approval by a director is required for any single expense above 5000 USD.

## 6. Procurement and supplier spend

- Purchases above 10000 USD require three written supplier quotes.
- Purchases above 50000 USD require a competitive tender reviewed by Procurement.
- A purchase order must exist before any supplier invoice is accepted.

## 7. Security and data handling

- Business documents classified as internal must not be stored on personal devices.
- Customer contact details are restricted data and may not be exported outside the
  managed export area.
- Suspected data exposure is reported to the security desk within one business day.
